OUR IDEA

All things PH,
one place.

OneStop PH is a lifestyle and discovery website concept designed to make local recommendations easier to explore.

WHY ONESTOP PH?

Discover more. Explore local.

The Philippines has so much to offer, from neighborhood food spots and independent films to music, games, destinations, and community events. OneStop PH brings these interests together in one simple, accessible place.

This project demonstrates the use of HTML for structure, CSS for presentation, and JavaScript for interaction and client-side validation.
